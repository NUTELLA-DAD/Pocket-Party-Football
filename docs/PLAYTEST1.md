# Playtest 1 (fill this in on the night)

30 minutes, 4 to 8 people, phones on the same Wi-Fi as the laptop, the TV (or laptop screen) about 2.5 m away. Nobody gets coached: say only "scan the code on the TV".

## Before people arrive (5 minutes)
1. Wi-Fi on the laptop set to **Private** (Settings > Network & internet > Wi-Fi > your network > Private).
2. Double-click **Play.bat**. The first time, Windows asks about the firewall: tick Private networks and click Allow.
3. Check one phone can join (scan the QR code), then leave the game running.
4. Have a stopwatch ready (a phone timer is fine), and this sheet.

## What to watch (tick or write a number)
- [ ] Time from scanning the QR to the first snap: ______ (target: under 2 minutes)
- [ ] Did anyone work out the other team's call from the TV? ______ (target: no)
- [ ] "Where am I?" moments per person: ______ (target: under 2)
- [ ] Could the QB throw to the receiver they meant to? ______
- [ ] Did tackles feel fair? Did a JUKE ever work? ______
- [ ] Anyone unsure what a button does? Which button? ______
- [ ] The huddle clock (15 s): too short / right / too long? ______
- [ ] Did anyone put the phone down and get a bot ("(BOT)" on the TV)? ______

## Ask at the end (one line each)
- Would you play again next week? ______
- The one thing to change: ______
