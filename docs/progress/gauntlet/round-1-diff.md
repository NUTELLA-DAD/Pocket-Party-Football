# Gauntlet round 1: concept04 (in-game camera) vs ours

Worst first.

1. **Camera too close.** The concept shows about 46 yards across (both "30"s at the picture edges, 40→50 = 340 px on the far row, 400 px on the near row). Ours shows about 36 (440 / 505 px). The field also fills about the same height, so the concept camera looks down a bit more steeply: about 19° instead of 16°.
2. **Players too small once the camera widens.** Concept QB is about 110 px tall at this width. Ours matches now but will shrink by 1.27x with the wider view, so the figures need to be about 1.27x bigger (2.9 m "chibi" scale).
3. **The far stands and crowd hardly show.** The concept's top band (above the far sideline) is a packed, colourful crowd behind ad boards. Ours shows the far sideline people and dark tent shapes, with no crowd visible under the scoreboard.
4. **Faces turned away.** Concept figures face 3/4 toward the camera (faces, eyes and numbers visible). Ours are nearly side-on (about 28° toward the camera).
5. **The ball is far too big** in the QB's hand (bigger than his helmet). The concept ball is about a third of a helmet.
6. **Helmet highlight is a big white blob.** The concept has a small, soft glint.
7. **Hash marks too close together.** The concept's two hash rows sit at about 1/3 and 2/3 of the field depth; ours are about 6 yards apart in the middle.
8. **Centre logo.** The concept's centre mark is a big bold "S" swoosh with white edging; our chevrons read like letters ("EU") beside the S.
9. **Near bench.** The concept's bottom edge shows bench players from the chest up, an orange marker and a ref. Ours shows only the tops of their heads.
10. Minor: the concept's grass has more painted texture; the line of scrimmage is a little thicker; the scoreboard bar is a little larger.
