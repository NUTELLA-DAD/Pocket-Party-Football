# Gauntlet round 3: concept04 vs ours

Fixed since round 2: the crowd now fills the top band, the centre mark is the right width, bigger yard numbers, deeper grass, scoreboard size.

Worst first:
1. **Far-sideline people too big.** In the concept the far sideline's players, coaches and refs are small (about 60% of a field player), a dense row under the crowd. Ours are full size and hide the sideline.
2. **Near bench on the white border.** The concept's bench players stand below the near sideline band (we see their heads and shoulders); ours stand on the band and cover it.
3. **Centre "S" reads as an outline.** The concept's S is a thick, filled shape with a white edge and big wheat on both sides; ours is a thin stroke.
4. **The line is a straight column.** The concept's linemen stagger, so the cluster looks like a scrum.
5. **The crowd is too multicoloured and blocky.** The concept's crowd is a fine, mostly green texture with some red and white.
