# Couch Bowl

An 11-on-11 toon football party game (Arrows vs Grains) for one TV. Everyone plays with their phone: scan the QR code, pick a side and a position, call plays in secret, and play with position-specific buttons. Bots fill every empty spot.

- The plan: [docs/PLAN.md](docs/PLAN.md)
- The first build jobs: [docs/BUILD_BRIEF.md](docs/BUILD_BRIEF.md)
- Season mode (recruits, coins, coaching, names on jerseys): [docs/SEASON.md](docs/SEASON.md)
- The phone controller mockup: [docs/mockup/controller.html](docs/mockup/controller.html) (open in a browser)

Built in Unity 6 LTS (URP). The phone-controller design comes from Airship Crew (github.com/NUTELLA-DAD/QR-PHONE-GAME, `UNITY.md`).
